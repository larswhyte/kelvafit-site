Train

Every class has a plan. Every set gets logged.

Coaches plan each class once: warm-up, supersets and tri-sets. Members see it before they arrive, log their sets and weights, and the whole class shows up on the gym TV.

  • Class plans with warm-ups, supersets and tri-sets
  • Members log sets, reps and weights, with last time's weights filled in
  • Film exercise demos on your own equipment, so members see your coaches and your kit, not stock clips
  • Personal bests tracked by reps and weight, not just the heaviest lift
  • The Class Board: cast the class to the TV, and sets turn green as members finish them
  • Booking, waitlists and a weekly view of each member's training
A plan for every member

Build your own programmes and meal plans, for every member

You and your coaches build the training programmes in your own library, plus meal plans if you offer nutrition, then give them to any member, not just PT clients. They follow them in the app, and you see how they're getting on.

  • Build multi-week training programmes and assign them to any member
  • Set daily nutrition targets, with separate training-day and rest-day numbers
  • Build 7-day meal plans with your own recipes, photos and method, for members to follow meal by meal
  • Build once in your library, reuse for every member
  • See each member's progress: workouts done, days on target
Fuel

Nutrition that lives next to the training

Members log food in the same app they book and train in. Coaches see whether they're hitting their targets, without asking for screenshots.

Optional. Nutrition stays off until you switch it on. If you just want to run a gym, leave it off and your members never see it.

  • A food diary with calories, protein, carbs and fat against their targets
  • Snap a plate: take a photo of a meal to log it
  • Scan a barcode, or search the food list
  • Water tracking in a tap
  • Recipes and saved meals for the foods they eat often
  • Coaches see days on target, protein and water at a glance
Progress

Progress your members can see

Seeing the numbers move keeps people coming back. Members see it on their phone, and their coach sees the same.

  • Personal bests, streaks and a running total of everything they've lifted
  • Body weight and measurements over time, with a 7-day average
  • Muscle split: which muscles they train most, on a body map
  • Coaches see each member's sessions, streak, volume and PBs, plus where they're stalling and what to try next
  • An opt-in leaderboard built around effort: attendance, consistency, most improved and new PBs, by week, month or six months
  • Members can connect third-party fitness apps, so their workouts show up there too

FAQ

Questions gym owners and trainers ask

I'm a personal trainer without a gym. Does it work for me?

Yes. You can run KelvaFit for just yourself and your clients: session credits, programmes, nutrition targets and booking, with your own logo and colours.

Do my members need to download a new app?

Members get the app on iPhone or Android and join your gym. Inside, they see your logo and colours.

Can we have our own app in the App Store?

Yes, as an optional extra. We can publish an app under your gym's own name in the App Store and Google Play. Most gyms start with the standard app in their own colours.

We just run a gym. Do we have to use the nutrition side?

No. Nutrition is off until you switch it on in your gym settings, and while it's off your members don't see it. Classes, booking, training, progress and payments all work without it, and you can turn it on later if you want to.

Does KelvaFit come with ready-made programmes or meal plans?

No. KelvaFit gives you the tools, and you and your coaches write the programmes, meal plans and recipes. The exercise library and food database help you build them quickly, and your plans stay yours.

Is there a separate app for trainers?

No. Members, trainers and admins use the same app, and each sees the tools for their role. The coach web app at app.kelvafit.com is there when you want a bigger screen.

How do payments work?

Memberships and class packs are paid by card in the app, through Stripe.

Can you move our existing members across?

Yes. We help bring your existing members, plans and contacts across, so switching doesn't mean starting from zero.

Where are you based?

In Dublin, Ireland. We set up every gym ourselves, by call and email, and you talk directly to the people who build KelvaFit.

Is our gym's data kept separate?

Every gym gets its own private space. Your members, classes and results are only visible to your gym's staff, and members choose whether to appear on the leaderboard.

Does KelvaFit use AI?

In a few places where it saves time: recognising the foods in a photo of a plate, drafting a programme for a coach to check and edit, and answering members' questions about their own training. Coaches stay in charge of what members are given.
